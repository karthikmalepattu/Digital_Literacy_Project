## 📋 Module Summaries

### Module 1 – Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic
Created a single-page visual infographic covering the core pillars of digital literacy — what it means, useful tools for students, safe internet practices, professional online presence, and email etiquette. Designed using Canva with a clean, structured layout suitable for sharing with batchmates.

**Tool used:** Canva | **Format:** PNG export

### Module 4 – Professional Email & Etiquette
Drafted two professional emails — one requesting an assignment deadline extension from a professor, and one expressing interest in a summer internship to a company coordinator. Also created a Social Media Do's and Don'ts checklist specifically relevant to college students.

**Deliverables:** 2 email drafts · 1 checklist (10 items)

### Module 5 – Cybercrime Awareness
Researched and wrote a detailed case study on **UPI/Online Payment Fraud** — one of the most common cybercrimes targeting college students in India. Developed an 8-point prevention checklist with specific financial safety tips and official reporting channels.

**Cybercrime type covered:** UPI / Online Payment Fraud
**Reporting channel:** [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) | Helpline: **1930**

## 🎯 Why This Repository Exists

This portfolio was built as part of CSE0001 – Digital Literacy, but it's also the foundation of something bigger: a four-year plan to build a career in Cyber Security and AI. Every task here — from the GitHub profile to the cybercrime case study — is a real skill, not just an exercise.

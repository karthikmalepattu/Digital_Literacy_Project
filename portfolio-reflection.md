## Reflection 

I chose GitHub, LinkedIn, and Kaggle because together they cover the three most important dimensions of a career in technology — what you build, who you know, and what you can prove.

GitHub made the most immediate sense to me. In Cyber Security and AI, your code is your resume. A well-maintained GitHub profile with real projects will say more about me to a recruiter or a research lab than any certificate will. Setting up the profile README felt almost like writing a first introduction to the world.

LinkedIn was less obvious to me before this task. I always thought it was for people with five years of experience. But I realised that the earlier you establish your presence, the more organic your network grows — and that connections from first year often become the most valuable by the time placements arrive.

Kaggle rounds it out because it gives me a trackable record of practice. Every challenge attempted, every dataset explored — it creates a visible learning trail that complements the theory I'm studying in class.

Over the next four years, I plan to keep all three active: GitHub with real project code, LinkedIn updated after each significant achievement, and Kaggle as my space to experiment and compete.
